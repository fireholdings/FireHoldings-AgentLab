## Dropped (with reason)

- Generic AI automation agency, AI audits, white-label voice receptionist: commoditized, documented agency shutdowns.
- Corporate Transparency Act filings: obligation removed for domestic companies (Aug 2026).
- California workplace-violence plans: past deadline, free state template, $149 packages.
- Public adjusting, property-tax appeals, customs classification, unclaimed property, ERC: licence or credential required.
- Contingency audits (freight, CAM, telecom): 60–180 days to cash, fails the 30-day test.
- Medical billing / prior auth / credentialing: PHI needs a BAA not available on the Pro plan (credentialing alone is possible but slow: payers take 60–120 days).
- Tariff-refund prep: perishable window, crowded, status of later phases unconfirmed.
- Deal sourcing on success fee: broker-licensing risk; searchers report poor results with paid lists.
- Buying a business: SBA rules still need about 10% equity; not possible on $1,000.
- GSA schedule consulting, SBIR writing, per-bid proposal writing: long cycles or policy volatility.
- PI medical chronologies, lease abstraction, SaaS audits: AI tools already price near zero.
