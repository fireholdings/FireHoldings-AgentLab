## Weakest inputs and cheap ways to verify
1. **Annual and fleet-size counts for B2.** The 1-5 truck share and annual new-USDOT count are not published directly. Download the FMCSA Motor Carrier Census file (free, FMCSA Open Data) and count by power units and add date; this is a few hours of work.
2. **New-entrant failure rate.** The 91.4% pass rate and the 15-20% failure claim conflict. Pull the FMCSA New Entrant program data or the progress reports, and check whether "failure" means first audit or final outcome.
3. **B1 pricing and who buys.** No provider publishes writing or maintenance prices. Fetch five or six consultant websites and the ISN pricing calculator, and read public review threads, with no contact needed. Check how many small contractors actually use outside help versus doing it themselves.
4. **Churn and retention.** Every figure is generic SMB SaaS. A 3-5% monthly assumption for these niches is a HYPOTHESIS; test it with a pilot cohort.
5. **Valuation of $0.5-3M EBITDA firms.** No transaction evidence found. BizBuySell and DealStats market data (paid, small fee) are the right sources. Treat the 12.3x sector figure as irrelevant for a firm this small.
6. **Competitor size.** JJ Safety revenue estimates differ by 4x or more. LinkedIn headcount trends and state business filings give a better read than aggregators.
7. **Staffing costs.** Remote admin wages and software costs were not researched. Pull BLS OEWS data for office/admin and price a typical stack directly.

Not covered: Foley, Growjo data, OOIDA, ATRI and FMCSA revocation counts were not found in the searches run.
