## 2. Top 3 in depth

### A. Prequalification onboarding + safety programs (rows 1, 2, 12)
- **Real pricing:** The platform fee is borne by the contractor. ISN: about $875/yr for smaller firms, $680 for 1-4 employees up to $17,000 for 2,500+, based on a three-year average headcount (howmuchisit.org, ustechautomations.com; both undated, third-party). Avetta: $450-$900 per supplier per year per one estimate (erpresearch.com, undated); older Honeywell program $409 one-time fee (yespress.io). Veriforce: no reliable pricing found.
- **Complaints (VERIFIED but anecdotal):** One GetApp reviewer called ISN hard to update and the most expensive; another said subcontractors carry the burden (getapp.ca, undated). SoftwareAdvice review of Veriforce: 1.2/5 value, "does not return phone calls" (single review). A competitor blog cites 38% of subs naming fees as a barrier, attributed to AGC (vendor source, unverified).
- **Who pays and why fast:** A sub told by a hiring client to get approved before mobilizing has a hard deadline. HYPOTHESIS: two-week buying is plausible when the sub has a start date.
- **Prospect sources:** Hiring-client contractor lists and "approved contractor" pages, state contractor license databases, Santee Cooper and SRP joining ISN (safetyservicescompany.com blog, 2026) signals utilities onboarding new subs. Public license lists are the main source; a lead list is not an ISN/Avetta data product.
- **What kills it:** Incumbent consultants undercut; the approval depends on the hiring client's reviewer, so a guarantee is risky; liability if safety content is wrong; platform terms may restrict third parties from logging in.

### B. Certified payroll for small subs (row 3)
- **Pricing:** $50-$150/report or $200-$1,000+/job, $5-$15/employee/period (provider-pricing guide, undated, not a buyer-side quote). Internal-cost benchmark: about 55 min per report at $35/hr burdened (imagetotable.ai, undated, assumption-based).
- **Complaints:** Not found. I found no first-hand complaints about outsourced certified payroll providers; evidence gap.
- **Prospect sources:** Wage determinations are on SAM.gov; I could not confirm a downloadable list of Davis-Bacon contractors or subcontractor reports (the SAM.gov data bank has "Subcontract Reports" but scope unverified). FPDS award data requires a SAM account to use (sam.gov, undated). State DOT bid tabs and prevailing-wage project lists are likely sources (HYPOTHESIS).
- **Why pay in two weeks:** Weekly deadline recurs; one missed report can delay a pay application. HYPOTHESIS.
- **What kills it:** Payroll-data handling and error liability; software (Criterion, LCPtracker) is cheap; relies on subs sharing payroll exports.

### C. Lien waiver + pay-app + closeout administration (rows 4, 5)
- **Pricing:** Software only: LienIt about $2,400-$6,000, Textura $24,000-$96,000 (2026 comparison article, header ambiguity). Vendor claims 30-60 min saved per pay app (Flashtract) and 316 hrs/yr (Built); both are marketing.
- **Complaints:** None verified. Evidence gap.
- **Prospects:** State licensing boards, local plan-room and bid lists, ENR/regional GC lists (HYPOTHESIS).
- **Why two weeks:** Payment is held until waivers arrive. HYPOTHESIS.
- **What kills it:** Contractors do not hand over billing access; state-specific statutory waiver forms; services are hard to scale beyond hours; thinnest evidence of the three.

## 3. Founder fit (honest)
- Helps: regulated-environment discipline, safety/security program management and training-system building map directly onto rows 1, 2, 11, 12 (written programs, audit-ready records). NNSA program-management experience is credible to hiring-client safety managers. HYPOTHESIS: Army SF/federal-contract credibility helps on federal Davis-Bacon work (row 3), but does not make the buyer a customer.
- Does not help: restoration/Xactimate, solar permitting and estimating require trade domain knowledge and licences or software he lacks; answering service is commodity. Construction buyers do not know him, so he needs a warm-intro or referral path, which this research could not assess.
- Exit: A $5M+ sale of an owner-dependent service is unlikely (ESTIMATE). It needs a productised, recurring-revenue, systemised offering (for example a compliance-subscription), and I found no deal comps to cite.

## Bottom line
Best fit by evidence and background: A (prequal + safety programs), then B. Next test: price a fixed-fee package and check whether platform terms allow third-party help before any outreach.
