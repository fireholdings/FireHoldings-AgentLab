## Key corrections to the premise
- **Corporate Transparency Act: dead for domestic companies. Do not build on it.** VERIFIED: FinCEN final rule issued Aug 11, 2026, published Aug 14, 2026, permanently exempts US-formed entities and US persons; only certain foreign entities registered in the US remain covered (Harvard Law corpgov 2026-08-27, https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2026/08/27/fincen-permanently-eliminates-boi-reporting-requirements-for-us-companies-and-us-persons/; Mayer Brown 2026-08-20).
- **PFAS TSCA 8(a)(7): delayed again.** VERIFIED: reporting window now begins Jan 31, 2027 or 60 days after a forthcoming substantive rule, whichever is earlier (Faegre Drinker 2026-04-13; Clifford Chance 2026-05). Manufacturer/importer-heavy; poor fit for a solo operator.
- **California SB 553 is past due.** VERIFIED: compliance date was July 1, 2024; Cal/OSHA's model plan is free and optional (search result, dir.ca.gov model plan). Free templates exist (CaseIQ, Goethena); $149 one-time packages exist. Commodity, not urgent.
- **Colorado AI Act delayed to Jan 1, 2027 and scaled back** (SB 189 signed May 14, 2026, per Hunton). Narrow disclosure focus; thin demand for a solo shop.
- **NSGP FY2026 window closed** (state deadlines ~July 10-15; federal July 24, 2026; $300M, up to $200K/site per grantedai.com blog, secondary). Next cycle likely mid-2027; HYPOTHESIS.

## Part 2: Top 3 deep evidence

### Top 1: New-entrant trucking compliance (#1, with #2 and #3 as upsells)
- **Demand mechanism:** every new USDOT/MC registrant gets a New Entrant Safety Audit in the first 12 months and 18 months of monitoring (VERIFIED, simplexgroup.net 2026 guides; truenorth.com). The audit is free from FMCSA (single secondary source). Audits reviewing at least five areas: driver qualification, HOS, maintenance, accident register, drug/alcohol (VERIFIED).
- **Lead source:** Company Census File on data.transportation.gov (dataset ID az4n-8mr2, filterable by add_date) per third-party Apify actor descriptions; an SMS census file (kjg3-diqy) reportedly has emails for about 55% (third-party, UNVERIFIED). Sources conflict on update frequency (daily vs monthly). FMCSA's own Downloads page is the official reference (catalog.data.gov). Many Apify "new carrier lead" scrapers already exist, which means competitors also have this list; speed and credibility are the differentiator.
- **Price evidence:** monitoring $45/mo; bundles $149-$400/mo; new-authority bundle $199/mo; MCS-150 $45; reinstatement $295 (all vendor pages, 2026). $1,000 in 30 days requires about 5-7 customers at $150-200, or 2-3 at a $400-500 audit-prep fee. HYPOTHESIS, no conversion data.
- **Penalty evidence:** Clearinghouse penalty originally $5,833/violation; current figures unverified. Primary source needed: 49 CFR Part 386 App. B and the latest Federal Register inflation notice.
- **Limits:** do not give legal opinions or represent a carrier in enforcement proceedings (UPL and FMCSA practice rules; ESTIMATE). Stay in document prep, calendar, filing.
- **Risk:** crowded, low price, churn when carriers fold. Sellable asset is a book of recurring contracts; $5M exit is plausible only with many hundreds of subscribers (HYPOTHESIS).

### Top 2: Fleet DQF/Clearinghouse/rule-change readiness for 5-50 driver fleets (#3, #4)
- Larger tickets per client (per-driver pricing $100/driver/mo at Trucksafe-like vendors; Trucking42 claim). 2026 rule churn (non-domiciled CDL rule effective Mar 16, 2026 with D.C. Circuit litigation and state AG challenges; ELP out-of-service enforcement) creates anxiety. FMCSA said non-domiciled drivers may keep driving until license expiry; reduction expected over about 5 years (VERIFIED secondary). This makes it a steady roster-management service, not a cliff.
- Gap: no cheap implementation-level service found in searches; law firms publish alerts only. Risk: discrimination law; sell documentation, not eligibility judgments.

### Top 3: OSHA/security program packages for small employers (#5/#6)
- Fits the founder's regulated-environment and security-program credentials best, and is the least crowded at the "site-specific, done in a day" level. But I have NO sourced penalty or price data from this session beyond SB 553 templates ($0 free to $149; $597/mo managed). Free state model plans cap price. Rank is a HYPOTHESIS; validate with penalty data (OSHA penalty schedule) and three published consultant price sheets before committing.
