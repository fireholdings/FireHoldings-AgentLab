# 09 - Professional services, logistics, procurement, CRE back-office opportunities

Researched 2026-11-11, about 14 searches, no page fetches. Search snippets only. Most price sources are vendor or affiliate blogs, mostly undated in the snippets, so treat figures as asking prices. Labels: VERIFIED = seen in a search result from a named source; ESTIMATE = my inference from those; HYPOTHESIS = untested. Two searches (EvenUp, first attempt) errored; I retried. Gaps are stated, not filled.

## Key evidence gathered (VERIFIED unless noted)
- PI medical chronology, per-page asks: $0.10/page AI plus medical verification; $0.29/page at 100k+ page tier falling to $0.25; CaseMark $25 + $0.01/page (lezdotechmed.com/casedrive/pricing.html; caseMark/ustechautomations roundup; dates not shown). Price per page is collapsing toward cents.
- EvenUp: no public rate card; moved to per-case pricing in May 2025 (legaltechnology.com "EvenUp launches AI Drafts Suite..."; businesswire 2025-05-14). Third-party guess of about $300 base demand, $500-800 with add-ons is ESTIMATE (unverified review sites). It uses human reviewers. No firm complaints found.
- SOC 2: Drata from about $6,500/yr and Vanta about $7,200/yr for 1-20 staff per reseller getagency.com; aggregator median $20k Vanta, $12k Drata (costbench.com, Mar 2026); readiness assessment $5k-$15k for under-50 staff (soc2auditors.org); consultant-led $40k-$150k (vendor claim). Questionnaire tools: Conveyor Business from $9,600/yr and Inventive AI from $899/mo (inventive.ai blog, undated); Vanta bundles questionnaire automation.
- Lease abstraction outsourced: about $75-$400/lease, other sources $200-$600, one firm $375-$450 (rets.ai, unframe.ai, springbord.com; 2026 vendor blogs).
- CAM audits: contingency commonly 25-50% of recovery; hourly $300-$500; average recovery 3-12% of CAM billed (vendor claims); audit windows 60-90 days to 12 months, lease-specific (beancount.io 2026-05-17; springbord.com).
- Freight audit: models are subscription, per-invoice, or contingency 15-25% of recovered; 3-7% of invoices contain errors (vendor claim); detention and liftgate most disputed (racklify.com, nuvocargo.com, 2026).
- Dispatch: 5-10% of load gross, single truck 6-8%, flat weekly fee alternatives (otrucking.com, superdispatch.com, numeo.ai; vendors).
- SaaS spend: Vendr Starter about $36k/yr (G2); Tropic from $10k/yr per SaaSworthy, aimed at $10M+ SaaS spend; a one-off productized audit at A$99 exists (intent-engine.pages.dev). Platforms ignore small firms below about $1M SaaS spend (ESTIMATE).
- RFP help: Bid Lab lists per-bid fixed fee, hourly, retainer, success fee (success fees restricted on federal contracts); grant-writer hourly $35-$75 and $75-$150+ (Thumbtack). No reliable per-RFP price found.
- Customs: 19 USC 1641 / 19 CFR 111 require a license for "customs business"; CBP ruled (reported 2023-12-21, internationaltradetoday.com) a broker cannot use an unlicensed firm to key entry data. Pure classification advice is unclear; a 2026 rule on foreign importers of record also exists (federalregister 2026-22735).
- Property tax: Texas consultants register with TDLR (Occ. Code ch. 1152); Illinois PTAB bars non-attorney consultants and requires an attorney for LLCs; contingency examples 10% to 40%.
- W-9/1099 chasing: many software tools (TaxBandits, Liscio, Soraban, Piko); no outsourced-service prices found. Notable: CPA Practice Advisor (2024-04-19) reports CAS firms adding 1099/W-9 services.

## Top 3 deeper evidence

**1. Tenant CAM audit plus lease abstraction (opps 1-2).** Fit: founder's program-management and document-rigor skills; the unit of work is lease versus statement, which AI can pre-screen. VERIFIED: contingency 25-50% and recovery 3-12% of CAM (vendor sources, 2026); audit windows of 60-90 days to 12 months means urgency is real. ESTIMATE: a multi-site tenant with $100k CAM per year and 5% overcharge yields $5k recovery and $1.5k fee at 30%. Risk: contingency revenue arrives late, so the $1,000-in-30-days goal is hard; mitigate with a fixed-fee $500-$1,000 lease-and-statement review for tenants. Prospect sources (HYPOTHESIS): franchise owner groups, state restaurant/retail association lists, LoopNet tenant listings, county business filings. Gap: Springbord and similar firms mention services but I found no small-tenant price. Complaint evidence: none found.

**2. Security questionnaire completion for startups (opp 4).** VERIFIED: tools cost $5k-$10k/yr, and readiness $5k-$15k, so a $500-$1,500 flat done-for-you service is cheap relative to a blocked deal. Customer pain is deal-driven (urgency). Weakness: tools bundle AI answers (Conveyor, Vanta), a service only wins if customers lack the tool or documents. Prospect sources (HYPOTHESIS): founders posting on public startup communities about SOC 2 or questionnaires, YC/accelerator alumni lists. Must disclose it is not an audit.

**3. Freight audit / accessorial recovery for small shippers and brokers (opp 7).** VERIFIED: 15-25% contingency, 3-7% invoice error rate (vendor claims), detention a common dispute. Unit economics ESTIMATE: $2M freight spend x 3% recovery = $60k, 20% fee = $12k. Gap: large auditors target big shippers; a small-shipper offering is plausible but unproven. Risk: data access to carrier invoices, slow recovery cash, and carrier disputes need evidence (BOLs, timestamps). Prospect sources (HYPOTHESIS): FMCSA public broker/carrier registry lists, shipper associations, LinkedIn logistics managers. Not verified: actual small-shipper willingness to pay.

## Looks good on paper, weak urgency or heavy AI substitution
- PI chronology and demands (3): per-page prices of $0.10-$0.29 and EvenUp's scale and human review make a solo operator a price-taker. Possible only as an attorney-supervised niche.
- Lease abstraction alone (2): $25 self-serve AI vs $75-$600 manual; commodity.
- SaaS spend audit (11): free audits (Spendbase) and A$99 productized audits; low contract value.
- Dispatch (9): crowded, trust-driven, 5-10% fees, AI dispatch already marketed; hard to sell later.
- Customs classification (10): licensing risk and unclear boundary; avoid entry-related work.
- Property tax (12): seasonal deadlines and state-specific representation limits (Illinois attorney rule); slow cash.
- W-9/1099 chasing (13): software is abundant; value per client is small outside January.
- Fractional ops/SOP (15) and HOA admin (14): not enough evidence gathered; HYPOTHESIS only.

## Bottom line
For fast revenue with a defensible path: fixed-fee reviews (CAM/lease statement review, security questionnaire completion, freight invoice audit) sold before building anything, with contingency upsell. Exit to $5M+ requires turning one into a repeatable productized service with software; no acquirer evidence was found beyond category guesses. I did not research fractional COO, HOA admin pricing, or procurement/spend analysis beyond SaaS; those remain open.
