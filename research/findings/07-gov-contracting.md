# 07 - Government-Adjacent Services for Small Business (research as of 2026-11-11)

Method: about 12 web searches. Two page fetches failed (network allowlist blocked info.winvale.com and www.sledai.com), so most price data comes from search-result summaries of vendor sites. Labels: VERIFIED = a search result stated it (I did not open most pages); ESTIMATE = my inference; HYPOTHESIS = untested. Several searches rejected or returned nothing (rate limit once). Not covered at all: state/local bid-service pricing, FOIA competitive intelligence, 8(a) 2026 changes, HUBZone, Buy American/tariff compliance. I have no data on these and invent none.

## Key 2025-2026 facts
- SAM.gov registration is free. Fee-charging "registration departments" are a documented scam pattern: BBB Scam Tracker entries (late 2025, Jan 12 2026), a Nebraska DOT flyer (July 2026), GSA OIG alerts, and Woods Rogers/JD Supra commentary. VERIFIED (search summary). Stigma is real; any paid offer must be framed as advisory, never "registration for a fee."
- SBIR/STTR lapsed Sept 30 2025, reauthorized by P.L. 119-83 (S.3971) on April 13 2026 through FY2031. It adds security-risk screening, per-firm proposal caps starting FY2027, and a Strategic Breakthrough Phase II category. VERIFIED (CRS IN12705, 2026-07-13: everycrsreport.com; JD Supra; Mondaq).
- DOT DBE/ACDBE interim final rule effective Oct 3 2025 removed race/sex presumptions. Every certified firm must be re-evaluated and show disadvantage individually, and DBE participation could not be counted toward goals until recertification. VERIFIED (JD Supra, Goodwin Oct 2025, DOT guidance 9-30-2025 PDF). This creates a short-term recertification-help market, but it is legally contested.
- SBA VetCert (SDVOSB/VOSB): SBA said in Nov 2025 it cleared a 2,700+ application backlog, with average processing falling from 81 to 12 days. Certification is free, valid 3 years, and renewal opens 90 days before expiry. VERIFIED but advocacy and consultant sources (military.com 2025-11-13; executivegov). Free and fast means little room to charge for filing alone.
- FAR overhaul ("Revolutionary FAR Overhaul"): the FAR Council began formal rulemaking in June 2026 and published 17 parts for comment. Part 8 moved Federal Supply Schedule ordering into GSAR 538.71. MAS Refresh #34 is expected Nov 2026. MAS consolidation is merging contractors' multiple contracts into one. VERIFIED (Winvale, MeriTalk, CGP, PilieroMazza; consultant sources). One consultant article claims a June 30 2026 small-business update deadline; I could not confirm it.
- GSA flagged a "glitch" that may have corrupted small-business representations in some SAM registrations (Crowell; sources disagree on the date, 2024 vs 2026). A possible paid "SAM representations audit" hook, UNVERIFIED as to scale.
- Demand: Washington Technology reader poll (n=52, April 2025): 82.4% had a cancellation, delay or termination, but about 70% expected under 10% revenue impact. Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses survey (n about 1,500, Sept 23-Oct 2 2025): 16% said they are federal contractors/subs expecting revenue impact. Deltek: the pool of small federal contractors is shrinking while dollars grow, so more competition per prime award. VERIFIED (search summaries). No rigorous subcontractor-demand data found. Claims like "$41B cancelled" come from marketing posts and should not be used.

## (2) Top 3 (ranked by speed to $1,000 and scalability)

**A. Fixed-fee bid/no-bid + compliance review (#1).** ESTIMATE: 2-3 sales of $500 hit $1,000. The published range ($3k-$25k per bid; $150-$400/hr) means a $500-$1,500 entry priced far below an anchor looks sensible. Evidence: GovConInABox and OST Global say small businesses usually start per-bid fixed fee, and that scope comparisons differ widely. Source dates not captured. Gap: low-priced, fast, ex-military/NNSA compliance discipline. Scale path: productize into a monthly "pursuit desk" retainer; a retainer hybrid of $3k-$5k/mo plus 2-5% of awards appears in one source, but federal success fees are restricted per another, so avoid that. Failure mode: customers want a writer, not a reviewer; AI tools compress the price.

**B. SAM health check + capability/targeting package (#3 + #4).** The market is real because scams target exactly this audience, so trust is the scarce commodity. Prospect lists: SAM Entity Management extracts and API are free with an api.data.gov key (open.gsa.gov), with monthly snapshots; I found no confirmed "new registrant" filter, so diffing snapshots is my inference. USAspending API: nothing confirmed in my searches (HYPOTHESIS that it's free and keyless). Risk: if a buyer confuses you with the scammers, reputation damage. Mitigation: state upfront that registration is free, never cold-contact in a way that mimics SAM notices, and keep the paid scope as analysis only. A $99-$250 audit needs 5-10 sales for $1,000. Weak exit value alone; it feeds B into A.

**C. Contract data/lead product (#11).** Build from free public data, sell a sample and subscription to GovCon vendors. Highest AI leverage and best 3-5 year equity story (recurring revenue), but the first $1,000 is slower and competitors (large GovCon data vendors) are well funded. Treat as the scalable second layer behind A and B. No acquisition comps found; "$5M+" for any of these is HYPOTHESIS.

## (3) Honest risks
- **AI commoditization:** vendor blogs (GovEagle, SLED AI, Granted AI) already sell AI proposal and bid tools and frame consultants as replaceable (marketing sources, biased). Pure writing is the most exposed; compliance judgment, past-performance credibility and relationships are less so.
- **Scam stigma:** SAM-related paid services share a hostile search results page (BBB, GSA OIG, state agencies). Any outreach copy referencing "registration" or "renewal" can get flagged.
- **Policy volatility:** FAR rewrite is mid-rulemaking; MAS refreshes change quarterly; DBE rule is an interim final rule with APA challenge risk; SBIR now has proposal caps and security screening; shutdown (Oct 2025) and DOGE cancellations hit small and non-male-owned firms hard (advocacy source: USWCC). Customers with shrinking revenue buy less, though some need help re-pivoting (HYPOTHESIS).
- **Data gaps:** consultant prices are vendor-published and marketing-driven; SLED pricing, FOIA intel products, HUBZone and 8(a) changes, and acquirer multiples are unresearched. A $5M exit for a services business in this niche is hard (ESTIMATE); services trade at low multiples, so the realistic path is productizing into data/subscription before 3-5 years.
- **Legal:** do not give legal advice (DBE, False Claims exposure on GSA offers), do not guarantee awards, and do not scrape SAM.gov (prohibited, access restricted).
